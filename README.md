# Supportify
Supportify is a modern crowdfunding platform that enables individuals and communities to raise funds for meaningful causes through a secure and user-friendly digital experience. The platform allows users to create fundraising campaigns, receive donations, and manage campaigns efficiently with integrated AI assistance for improving campaign descriptions.

 Overview

Supportify is built to simplify online fundraising while ensuring transparency, accessibility, and ease of use. Users can create campaigns for social causes, education, medical emergencies, startups, or community projects, while donors can securely contribute and track campaign progress.

The platform also integrates an AI-powered rewrite assistant to help campaign creators generate clearer, more engaging campaign descriptions.
